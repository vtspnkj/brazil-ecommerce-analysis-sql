# Key Business Insights

## Executive Summary

This project analyzed a Brazilian e-commerce dataset using SQL in Google BigQuery to understand customer behavior, sales performance, logistics, and payment trends. The analysis generated several business insights that can help improve operational efficiency and customer satisfaction.

---

# Sales Performance

### 📈 Continuous Business Growth

- Order volume increased significantly from 2017 to 2018.
- Even with only January to October data for 2018, total orders exceeded the full-year total for 2017.
- This indicates strong year-over-year business growth.

**Recommendation**

- Continue investing in customer acquisition while strengthening customer retention programs.
- Scale operational capacity to support continued growth.

---

### 📅 Monthly Seasonality

- Monthly order volume fluctuated throughout the year.
- Certain months consistently experienced higher purchasing activity.

**Recommendation**

- Plan inventory based on seasonal demand.
- Schedule marketing campaigns before high-demand periods.
- Allocate warehouse staff based on expected order volume.

---

# Customer Analysis

### 🌎 Strong Geographic Coverage

- Customers placed orders from thousands of cities across all Brazilian states.
- The company has a broad national presence.

**Recommendation**

- Expand marketing efforts in cities with lower customer penetration.
- Continue strengthening nationwide coverage.

---

### 🏆 São Paulo Dominates Customer Base

- São Paulo (SP) has the largest number of customers.
- Other large states also contribute significantly to total sales.

**Recommendation**

- Prioritize warehouse expansion in high-demand states.
- Continue loyalty programs in top-performing regions.

---

# Customer Purchasing Behavior

### ☀ Afternoon is the Peak Shopping Period

- Most customers place orders during the afternoon.
- Early morning hours have the lowest purchasing activity.

**Recommendation**

- Schedule promotional campaigns during peak shopping hours.
- Increase customer support availability during busy periods.

---

# Revenue Analysis

### 💰 Order Value Differs by State

- States such as São Paulo and Rio de Janeiro generate the highest total order value.
- Several smaller states have lower overall spending.

**Recommendation**

- Focus premium product campaigns on high-value states.
- Introduce targeted promotions in lower-performing regions.

---

### 💳 Spending Increased Significantly

- Total customer spending increased substantially from 2017 to 2018 (January–August comparison).

**Recommendation**

- Maintain product availability.
- Improve fulfillment capacity during growth periods.

---

# Freight Analysis

### 🚚 Freight Costs Vary Across States

- Remote states generally experience higher average freight costs.
- High-volume states tend to have lower average freight costs due to economies of scale.

**Recommendation**

- Optimize warehouse locations.
- Improve shipping routes.
- Evaluate third-party logistics in high-cost regions.

---

# Delivery Performance

### 📦 Delivery Speed Differs by Region

- Delivery times vary considerably across Brazilian states.
- Some states consistently receive faster deliveries.

**Recommendation**

- Benchmark high-performing regions.
- Apply successful logistics strategies to slower regions.

---

### ⏱ Many Orders Arrive Before Estimated Delivery Date

- Several states consistently deliver earlier than the estimated delivery date.

**Recommendation**

- Reduce delivery estimates where operational performance consistently exceeds expectations.
- Improve customer satisfaction by providing more accurate delivery estimates.

---

# Payment Analysis

### 💳 Credit Cards are the Preferred Payment Method

- Credit cards account for the largest share of transactions.

**Recommendation**

- Continue supporting installment payments.
- Negotiate lower payment processing fees where possible.

---

### 💵 Customers Prefer Short Installment Plans

- Most customers choose one, two, or three payment installments.

**Recommendation**

- Offer attractive low-interest installment options.
- Promote financing for higher-value purchases.

---

# Overall Business Recommendations

- Improve warehouse distribution in high-demand regions.
- Reduce freight costs through logistics optimization.
- Increase inventory before seasonal demand peaks.
- Expand marketing in lower-performing states.
- Improve delivery efficiency using best-performing regions as benchmarks.
- Strengthen customer retention initiatives to support long-term growth.
- Continue monitoring payment behavior to optimize checkout experience.

---

# Conclusion

The analysis demonstrates consistent business growth, strong nationwide customer coverage, increasing customer spending, and opportunities to improve logistics, freight efficiency, and delivery performance. Implementing these recommendations can enhance operational efficiency, customer satisfaction, and long-term profitability.
